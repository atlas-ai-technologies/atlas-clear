1. About the application

ATLAS Clear is a Windows desktop utility providing local system status information, selected maintenance functions, folder backups, and restore operations.

2. Local information

The application may process system performance information, memory usage, storage details, process names, settings, and user-selected files or folders.

According to the documentation for version 2.2.0, application settings, reports, and backups are stored locally on the user's computer.

3. Data transmission

The documented version does not include application telemetry, advertising trackers, or automatic transmission of diagnostic reports to an ATLAS server.

Information voluntarily shared by the user outside the application is subject to the selected sharing service and its privacy practices.

4. Data security

Locally created backups are not encrypted by the application. Diagnostic JSON reports may contain process names and system information.

Users should review reports before sharing them and protect backup folders against unauthorized access.

5. Data retention and deletion

Local reports and backups remain on the user's device until they are deleted by the user or removed through applicable system cleanup procedures.

Uninstalling the application may not remove backups saved in separately selected folders.

6. Support communications

When users voluntarily contact support, their email address, message, and any provided attachments are processed to respond to the request and manage the related communication.

Support communications use Gmail, operated by Google, and may be processed according to applicable Google service terms and privacy arrangements.

Users may request information about their support data or ask for its deletion, subject to applicable legal retention obligations.

7. User rights

Where applicable under data protection law, users may request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of processing of personal data, and may object to processing or lodge a complaint with a competent data protection authority.

What the service processes

Microsoft Entra sign-in identifiers are used to derive an Atlas account identifier. Search identifiers, optional photos, source observations and report contents are processed to carry out the checks you request. Search inputs and stored report bodies are encrypted on the server. Credit balances and audit references are separate records. Search results may be inaccurate and are not identity verification.

Services and transfers

The Atlas backend runs on Oracle Cloud Infrastructure in Frankfurt, Germany. Microsoft Entra External ID handles account sign-in. Public-source checks send the relevant identifier to the source involved. The app asks for permission before starting checks. Optional web search, reverse-image search and cloud AI depend on enabled providers and separate availability; these are not promised as active during the test. A selected external provider may process data outside the European Economic Area; its contractual safeguards must be established before commercial activation.

Retention and deletion

Server reports expire after 30 days. Detailed page text is held temporarily in server memory for up to two minutes and is excluded from report exports and AI inputs. Exported .atlasdesk files are encrypted for the Windows profile that saved them and remain under your control. Account and privacy controls provide data export, stored-search erasure and account disabling. Financial, refund, security and anti-abuse references can survive account erasure; the final retention and backup-deletion schedule remains a commercial-release requirement. Contact the controller for a specific deletion or retention question.

Your choices and requests

You can refuse a search transfer, stop an active operation, and separately decline page reading or AI analysis.

Getting help

Include the application version, a short description of the problem and the non-secret reference shown by the app. Do not send passwords, API keys, sign-in codes, payment-card information, identity documents or full sensitive reports by email.

Search coverage

This release is for checking your own digital footprint. Results are source observations and possible matches. They do not prove identity, account ownership or wrongdoing. Phone metadata does not reveal a person's location; basic photo checks are not face recognition. Disabled or unavailable providers reduce coverage.

Planned billing

The planned model is a one-time app purchase plus credits: 5 credits per completed search and 5 per successfully read page. Detailed page reading is optional and needs separate price approval. No final euro prices are announced here. Purchases remain disabled during setup.

Reports and privacy

Server reports expire after 30 days. Export an encrypted copy if you need a longer record. Detailed page text is temporary and is not included in report exports or AI input. Contact support promptly about incorrect records, account access or privacy requests.
